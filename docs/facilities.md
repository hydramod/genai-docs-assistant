# Facilities

The members Wi-Fi network is called Fernhill-Members and runs at up to 1 Gbps. The password changes on the first Monday of every month and is shown on the screen at reception. A separate Fernhill-Guest network is available for signed-in guests.

Printing is in the print room on the ground floor. Resident and Dedicated Desk members get 50 free black-and-white pages per month. After that, black-and-white pages cost 5p each and colour pages cost 20p each. Flex members and Day Pass holders pay for every page at the same rates. Printing is charged to your account at the end of the month.

The kitchen has free tea, coffee and milk for all members. Please label any food you leave in the fridge with your name and the date. The fridge is cleared every Friday at 4pm, and anything left in it is thrown away.

There are 3 phone booths for private calls. When other people are waiting, please keep phone booth use to 30 minutes at a time. The whole second floor is a quiet zone: no phone or video calls, and please keep conversations to a whisper.
