# Membership

Fernhill Works is a coworking space for freelancers, small teams and remote workers. It's fictional and was written for this exercise. There are four ways to work here: Day Pass, Flex, Resident and Dedicated Desk. Every membership includes Wi-Fi, free tea and coffee, and access to the shared kitchen and lounge.

A Day Pass costs £18 and gives you a hot desk for one day during staffed hours. Day Passes can be bought at reception or in the Fernhill app, and they don't include meeting room credits or printing.

Flex membership costs £120 per month and gives you 10 days of hot-desking each month, plus 4 hours of meeting room credit. Unused days don't roll over to the next month. Students with a valid student ID get 20% off Flex membership.

Resident membership costs £220 per month and gives you unlimited hot-desking during staffed hours, 8 hours of meeting room credit per month, and 50 free black-and-white printed pages per month.

Dedicated Desk membership costs £310 per month. You get your own permanent desk, a lockable drawer unit, 24/7 access with a key fob, 12 hours of meeting room credit per month, and 50 free black-and-white printed pages per month.

Paying for a year upfront gives a 10% discount on Flex, Resident and Dedicated Desk memberships. The minimum term for any monthly membership is one month.

To cancel a membership, give 30 days' notice through the members portal. Fernhill Works doesn't refund partial months, so your membership runs until the end of the notice period. You can upgrade at any time and the new price starts from your next billing date. Downgrades also need 30 days' notice.
