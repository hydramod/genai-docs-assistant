# House rules and extras

Dogs are welcome on Fridays only. Each dog must be registered with reception first, and kept on a lead in shared areas. Dogs aren't allowed in the kitchen at any time.

There is secure bike storage in the basement with 20 spaces, on a first come, first served basis. There's no car parking on site. The nearest car park is Riverside car park, and Fernhill members get 15% off there by showing the Fernhill app at the ticket office.

Resident and Dedicated Desk members can use Fernhill Works as their registered business address, and reception will hold their post. Other members can add business address and post handling for £10 per month.

Every month there's a free members' breakfast on the first Wednesday at 8:30 in the lounge. It's a good way to meet the other people who work here.

Lost property is kept at reception for 30 days. To report a problem with the building or equipment, use the "Report an issue" button in the Fernhill app, or email hello@fernhill.example.com.
