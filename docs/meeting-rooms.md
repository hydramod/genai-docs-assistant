# Meeting rooms

There are three meeting rooms. Oak seats up to 4 people and has a wall screen. Birch seats up to 8 people and has a video conferencing setup with a camera and speakerphone. Cedar is the boardroom: it seats up to 14 people and has a projector, a whiteboard wall and video conferencing.

Meeting room credit comes with Flex (4 hours per month), Resident (8 hours per month) and Dedicated Desk (12 hours per month) memberships. Credit resets on the first day of each month and doesn't roll over.

Once your credit is used up, extra hours are charged per room: £12 per hour for Oak, £20 per hour for Birch and £35 per hour for Cedar. Day Pass holders can book rooms at these hourly rates too.

Rooms can be booked up to 14 days ahead in the Fernhill app. A single booking can be at most 4 hours long. To free a room up for others, cancel at least 2 hours before the start time. Bookings cancelled later than that, or not used at all, are still charged or taken from your credit.
