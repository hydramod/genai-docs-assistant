# Opening hours and access

Fernhill Works is staffed Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00, and on Saturdays from 10:00 to 16:00. The space is closed on Sundays and bank holidays for everyone except Dedicated Desk members.

Dedicated Desk members have 24/7 access using their key fob, including evenings, Sundays and bank holidays. A lost key fob costs £15 to replace. Report a lost fob straight away so the old one can be switched off.

Members can bring up to 2 guests per day for free, for up to 2 hours each. Every guest must be signed in at reception and stay with their host the whole time. A guest who stays longer than 2 hours needs a Day Pass.

Outside staffed hours, reception is closed and guests can't be signed in, so Dedicated Desk members can't bring guests in the evenings or on Sundays.
